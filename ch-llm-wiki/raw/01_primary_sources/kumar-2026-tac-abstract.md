# TAC (Timestamped Audio Captioning) — Abstract Snapshot

**Title:** Timestamped Audio Captioning: Probing Temporal Grounding in Large Audio-Language Models
**Authors:** Sonal Kumar et al. (NVIDIA + collaborators)
**arXiv ID:** 2602.15766 (17 Feb 2026)
**License:** CC BY 4.0
**Source URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.15766

## Abstract (verbatim, condensed — paraphrased per fetch)

Large audio-language models (LALMs) increasingly produce free-form descriptions of complex sound scenes, but their ability to localize sound events temporally — to know not just *what* happened, but *when* — remains poorly understood. We introduce Timestamped Audio Captioning (TAC), a benchmark and evaluation protocol for probing temporal grounding in LALMs. Using synthetic A-then-B mixtures with ground-truth onset labels, we evaluate state-of-the-art LALMs and find substantial gaps versus dedicated audio-event detection systems. We release the benchmark and code.
