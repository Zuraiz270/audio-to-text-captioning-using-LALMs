# PolyBench — Abstract Snapshot

## Abstract (paraphrased, retrieved 2026-04-20)

We introduce PolyBench, a benchmark for evaluating how large audio-language models (LALMs) describe and reason about polyphonic acoustic scenes — scenes containing multiple concurrent sound events. PolyBench includes five subsets stratified by event count, source separability, and ontology breadth, with paired captions and event-count labels. Evaluating contemporary LALMs reveals systematic under-description of concurrent events, confirming polyphony as a leading failure mode for current adapter-based audio-LLM architectures.
