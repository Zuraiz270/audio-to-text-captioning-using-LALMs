S. 4
Data in Machine Listening
Challenges 
•
Audio is “data-scarce” domain
• Technical complexity 
▪Audio is harder to scrape and extract than text or images
• Slower and more expensive annotation
▪Labels require listening in real-time
• Privacy risks 
▪Strict regulations create legal hurdles for data sharing
• Background noise requires costly filtering to ensure high-
quality training signals

S. 13
Data in Machine Listening
Data Protection
•
Audio as Personal Data
• Human speech contains biometric identifiers and PII 
(Personally Identifiable Information)
•
Privacy-Enhancing Technologies (PETs) 
• Audio pseudonymization (pitch shifting/voice masking) 
• Differential privacy to prevent "speaker re-identification" from 
the latent space
•
“Privacy-by-design" approaches
