## Abstract Summary

Proposes **TLAC** — a transfer learning-based audio caption generation method. Addresses the problem that traditional AAC methods require large amounts of labelled data via supervised learning, consuming significant human resources. Uses transfer learning to reduce labelling requirements.

(Source: IEEE Xplore abstract, IEEE-11199756)

## Key Contributions

- Transfer learning approach to reduce labelled data requirements for AAC.
- Addresses data scarcity — foundational problem in audio captioning.
