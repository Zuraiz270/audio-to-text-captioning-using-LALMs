## Abstract Summary

Comprehensive survey of Automated Audio Captioning (AAC). AAC mimics human perception by linking audio processing and NLP. Requires recognising environment, sound events, temporal relationships, and describing them in fluent sentences. Covers encoder-decoder architectures, training strategies, evaluation metrics, datasets, and open challenges.

(Source: IEEE Xplore / arXiv abstract, IEEE-10285526)

## Key Contributions

- Most comprehensive AAC survey at time of publication.
- Covers the full AAC pipeline: audio encoding → language decoding → evaluation.
- Establishes taxonomy of AAC approaches.
- Identifies key challenges: data scarcity, evaluation metric limitations, temporal grounding.

## Datasets Used

- Survey covering Clotho, AudioCaps, and other AAC datasets

## Metrics Reported

- Survey of all standard AAC metrics (BLEU, ROUGE, METEOR, CIDEr, SPICE, SPIDEr)

## Relevance to RQs

- **All RQs:** Foundational survey that contextualises every research question in this project.
- **RQ2 (Polyphony):** Identifies temporal relationships between events as a core AAC challenge.
- **Temporal Grounding Context:** Explicitly discusses temporal grounding limitations.

## Limitations / Gotchas

- Pre-LALM era survey (2023) — does not cover AF3, SALMONN, or Qwen2.5-Omni.
- Rapidly outdated by the LALM revolution.
