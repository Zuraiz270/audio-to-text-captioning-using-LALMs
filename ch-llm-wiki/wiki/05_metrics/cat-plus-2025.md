## Abstract Summary

Investigates a critical problem: MLLMs exhibit **"Ambiguity" bias** in dynamic audio-visual scenarios — generating nonsensical answers and making random guesses. This degrades both the model's ability to answer audio-visual questions and produce detailed, accurate responses. CAT+ aims to enhance AVQA accuracy by addressing this ambiguity in multimodal reasoning.

(Source: IEEE Xplore introduction, IEEE-11050020)

## Key Contributions

- Identifies and quantifies "Ambiguity" bias in MLLMs for AVQA tasks.
- Proposes methods to enhance audio-visual understanding in LLMs.
- Addresses the gap between visual QA performance and audio-visual QA performance.
