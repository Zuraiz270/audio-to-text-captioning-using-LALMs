## Abstract Summary

Applies Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) directly to Qwen2.5-Omni, achieving state-of-the-art results on MMAU and MMAR (Multi-Modal Audio Reasoning). A surprisingly profound conclusion: **fine-tuning the LLM on text-only reasoning tasks improves its audio reasoning almost as much as fine-tuning it directly on audio.**

(Source: Full PDF, IEEE-11434780)

## Key Findings

- **Architecture:** Built on Qwen2.5-Omni-7B.
- **Training Method (GRPO):** Eliminates explicit value functions (like in PPO) by sampling multiple outputs for a prompt and using their average reward as a baseline, saving massive GPU memory.
- **Prompt:** "<question> Please choose the answer from the following options: <choices>". Simple prompt enabled full-finetuning on 48GB GPUs.
- **The Text-Only Hypothesis:** The authors show that the massive gains achieved by Audio-LLMs post-RLHF (like R1-AQA and SARI) are primarily due to the LLM backend simply getting better at logical deduction, NOT because the model inherently understands audio better.

## Relevance to RQs

- **RQ3 (Hallucination):** Hallucinations in LALMs may not actually be "acoustic hallucinations", but rather linguistic logic breakdowns in the LLM backbone. If GRPO on text cures audio hallucinations, the problem was text-prior failure all along. This perfectly aligns with the findings in *Reducing Object Hallucination via Audio-Aware Decoding*.
- **Model Baselines:** Omni-R1 represents the peak of Qwen2.5's reasoning capabilities, surpassing standard Qwen2-Audio.
