## Abstract Summary

Investigates whether Retrieval-Augmented Generation can improve audio LLMs for low-resource languages — where training data is scarce or absent. Tests RAG as an amplification strategy for underserved languages.

(Source: IEEE Xplore metadata)

## Key Contributions

- First study of RAG for audio LLMs in low-resource language settings.
- Tests whether retrieval augmentation compensates for training data scarcity.
- Relevant to multilingual and cross-cultural audio understanding.
