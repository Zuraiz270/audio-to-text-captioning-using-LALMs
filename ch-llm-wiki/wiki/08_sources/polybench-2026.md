## Claim

Contemporary LALMs systematically under-describe concurrent sound events in polyphonic scenes. PolyBench provides a 5-subset benchmark stratified by event count, source separability, and ontology breadth, with paired captions and event-count labels.

## Method

- 5 stratified subsets
- Paired captions + event-count labels
- Multi-LALM evaluation
